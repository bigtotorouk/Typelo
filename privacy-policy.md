# Typelo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Typelo helps you save and reuse text and images through a snippet library, a custom keyboard, Collection, the share sheet, and Shortcuts.

This policy explains how Typelo handles your information.

## 1. Our Privacy Approach

Typelo works locally on your device. It does not require an account or include advertising, analytics SDKs, or AI services.

We do not upload your snippets, images, clipboard content, or typing to our servers. We do not sell your personal information or track you across apps and websites.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Typelo stores the information needed to provide its features, including:

- Text snippets, titles, and images you save.
- Categories, favorites, and recent snippet usage.
- Items you add to Collection.
- App preferences and cached Pro access status.

This information is stored locally. Typelo and its extensions use a shared app container to access the information needed for their features.

Typelo does not provide its own cloud storage or cloud synchronization.

## 3. Keyboard and Full Access

The Typelo keyboard uses your saved snippets to insert text or copy content when you choose.

Typelo does not record your keystrokes or build a history of what you type in other apps.

Some keyboard features require the iOS “Allow Full Access” setting, including actions that access the clipboard or update shared app data. Although this system permission can allow network access, the Typelo keyboard does not make network requests or transmit your content.

You can disable Full Access or remove the keyboard in iOS Settings. Some features will then be unavailable.

## 4. Clipboard, Sharing, and Shortcuts

Typelo accesses clipboard content when you explicitly paste or use a collection action. It does not automatically monitor clipboard changes.

The share extension imports content you choose to share with Typelo.

The Shortcuts action processes content supplied by a shortcut you configure. It may save that content while Typelo is not open, but it does not independently monitor or read the clipboard in the background.

Copying text or an image with Typelo replaces the current system clipboard content. Content you paste or share into another app is handled according to that app’s privacy practices.

## 5. Images

Typelo accesses images you select or explicitly provide through pasting, sharing, or Shortcuts. It does not request access to your entire photo library.

Imported images may be resized and re-encoded for storage and reuse. Typelo removes source location metadata from the processed image files it stores.

## 6. In-App Purchases

Typelo Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store.

Typelo uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to check purchase transactions and determine whether Pro features should be unlocked. Relevant transaction information and access status are processed on your device.

We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles payment information and App Store purchase records under its own privacy policy:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 7. Diagnostics

Typelo does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Limited technical information, such as content format identifiers and error codes, may be written to local system logs to help diagnose import failures. These diagnostic messages do not include your snippet text, image content, clipboard content, or typing.

Apple may separately process system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

## 8. Retention, Backups, and Deletion

Your saved content remains on your device until you delete it or it is otherwise removed.

You can delete individual items or use **Settings → Delete All Data** to remove Typelo’s library and Collection data.

Deleting data in Typelo does not clear content already copied to the system clipboard or remove content pasted, shared, or saved elsewhere. It also does not delete Apple’s purchase records.

Device backups may include Typelo data depending on your system settings. Backup retention and restoration are controlled by the applicable backup service. Removing the app may remove its local data.

## 9. Security

Typelo uses the app sandbox and shared-container protections provided by iOS. Access to your device and its backups also depends on your device security settings.

No method of storing information can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. External Links

Typelo may open external pages, including this privacy policy. When you visit an external website, its hosting provider may process ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy practices.

## 11. Information You Send Us

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message.

We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive snippets or clipboard content unless necessary for your request.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Typelo changes. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
