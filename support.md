# Typelo Support

Need help with Typelo? Contact us at **bigtotorouk@gmail.com**.

## Set Up the Keyboard

1. Open iOS **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard**.
2. Select **Typelo**.
3. Open a text field, touch and hold the globe key, and choose Typelo.

Some apps and secure text fields do not support third-party keyboards.

## Enable Full Access

Some keyboard features, including clipboard actions and updates to shared app data, require Full Access.

Go to **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Typelo** and enable **Allow Full Access**.

The Typelo keyboard does not make network requests or upload your content.

## Use Saved Snippets

Save text or images in the Typelo app, then switch to the Typelo keyboard.

- Tap a text snippet to insert it.
- Tap an image snippet to copy it, then paste it into an app that supports images.
- Touch and hold a snippet to preview it.

## Collect Content

Copy text or an image, then use Collect to add it to Collection. You can also share supported content to Typelo through the system share sheet.

Typelo does not automatically monitor your clipboard.

For Shortcuts setup, follow the guide inside Typelo. Connect **Get Clipboard** to Typelo’s **Collect Clipboard** action and pass the clipboard output to **Clipboard Input**.

## Restore Typelo Pro

Open the upgrade screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Typelo Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. If access is not restored, check your internet connection and try again.

Apple handles payments and refund requests. Visit:
https://reportaproblem.apple.com/

## Manage Your Data

Typelo stores your snippets and Collection locally. This version does not provide cloud sync or export backup.

To remove your saved content, open **Settings → Delete All Data** in Typelo. This cannot retract content already copied, pasted, or shared elsewhere.
